# What we need from Crown for the production system

The system's screens are ready to be designed, but its master data (the facts about your products, machines and people) must come from Crown. Please fill in what you can. A photo of a paper form or an Excel sheet is fine. Anything you are unsure about, mark "not sure" and we will ask.

Items marked **(start with)** unblock the first screens.

## A. Products (for each product: egg tray, jar cover, and any other you plan to make)

- [ ] **(start with)** Name, in English and in Amharic
- [ ] **(start with)** How it is counted and sold: pieces or kilograms
- [ ] **(start with)** Package size: how many pieces in one package (egg tray), or how many kg in one bag (jar cover)
- [ ] Weight of one piece in grams (jar cover is about 8 g; please confirm, and tell us the egg tray weight)
- [ ] Colours made for each product, in English and in Amharic (for example "clear")
- [ ] Any product code you already use

## B. Machines and moulds

- [ ] **(start with)** Each machine: its name or number, and which product it makes
- [ ] **(start with)** For each mould: number of cavities (the clear jar cap mould has 11; please confirm and give the others)
- [ ] **(start with)** Normal cycle time in seconds for each product and colour, and the usual range (the report shows 30.7 to 31.7 s)
- [ ] Planned production hours per shift (the report uses 14 hours)
- [ ] Does each machine run 24 hours? Any machine that does not?
- [ ] Mould or colour change: does it stop the machine, and for how long?

## C. Raw material and recipe (bill of materials) for each product and colour

- [ ] **(start with)** Material type and grade used (for example LLDPE, M500026)
- [ ] **(start with)** Material per piece in grams (about 8 g for the jar cap; please confirm and give the egg tray)
- [ ] Colour additive or masterbatch, and how much per kg of material
- [ ] Raw material bag size (we have 25 kg; please confirm)
- [ ] **Regrind (recycled damaged output):** may each product use regrind? From which products? Is there a maximum percentage? (Today: egg tray yes, jar cover no. We have no percentage yet.)
- [ ] Allowed difference from the recipe before an alert: we propose 5% (warning) and 10% (critical). Please confirm or change.

## D. Shifts

- [ ] **(start with)** Two shifts or three? Start and end time of each
- [ ] How the weekly rotation works (which team on day and which on night, and when it switches)
- [ ] Days off and public holidays the machines do not run
- [ ] Rest rotation: after how long on a machine does an operator move to QC? (We use 1 hour.)

## E. Reasons

- [ ] **(start with)** Reasons for damaged or rejected output. We start with the three in the weekly report: raw material spill from the mould, splitting, colour defect. Please add any others, in English and in Amharic.
- [ ] Reasons for machine downtime. The report shows "Power off". Please add others (machine breakdown, no material, mould change, cleaning, waiting for QC, and so on), in English and in Amharic.

## F. Stores

- [ ] Names you use for: raw material store, regrind store, QC area, staging area, finished goods store
- [ ] Minimum stock levels that should raise an alert, per product and for raw material (optional now)
- [ ] Alerts: how long idle counts as "too long"? What damage rate counts as "high"?

## G. People

- [ ] **(start with)** Everyone who will sign in: full name (English and Amharic), job title, and work email address. This includes the owner, production manager, shift production managers, QC inspectors, store managers, cashier, purchaser and finance.
- [ ] Who will be the system administrator at Crown (adds people and resets passwords)
- [ ] Operators do not sign in. For their records: full name, job title, usual shift or team.
- [ ] When operators later get accounts (HR module): do they have email, or should they sign in with a username or phone number?
